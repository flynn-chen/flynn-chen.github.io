Educational simulation for clinicians and supervised learners. Fictional patients; simplified, accelerated physiology. Not validated for clinical decisions. Treatment bundles omit dosing and require local protocols and specialist supervision in practice. All cases retain a pulse; cardiac arrest management is outside this simulation.
How to play & clinical references

Select a case, then choose the best next action at each of three decision points. Complete all three and maintain a score of at least 70 to pass. Incorrect choices cost 15 points and worsen perfusion; every 45 seconds spent on an unresolved decision costs 5 points. A MAP below 35 for 15 simulated seconds ends the case. Pause freezes the case; changing tabs automatically pauses. Monitor traces use an 8-second display window, not a calibrated paper speed. Pace accelerates case progression only; waveforms and sounds stay at the displayed rates. Sound must be enabled by a click. Optional browser speech reads handoffs and decision updates. No network connection is needed to play.

Clinical foundations: 2023 ACC/AHA/ACCP/HRS AF guideline; Vasoplegic syndrome following cardiothoracic surgery; Management of cardiac arrest following cardiac surgery (bleeding and tamponade background). Scenario values and scoring are authored teaching examples, not patient predictions.
